#  Bank Loan Credit Risk Analysis & Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing bank loan data to understand customer credit profiles, loan applications, repayment behavior, credit risk, and factors that may influence loan approval and risk.

The project combines data analysis with Power BI visualization to create an interactive credit risk dashboard that helps identify patterns in loan performance and customer risk.

---

## Business Problem

Banks need to evaluate loan applications carefully to reduce financial risk while providing loans to eligible customers.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

- How many loans are approved and rejected?
- Which customer segments have higher credit risk?
- How does credit score affect loan approval?
- Which employment types have higher loan risk?
- How does income influence loan eligibility?
- Which loan purposes are most common?
- How does the debt-to-income ratio affect risk?
- Which customer segments require additional attention?

##  Key Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Customer segments with higher or lower credit risk.
- Relationship between credit score and loan approval.
- Loan purposes associated with larger loan amounts.
- Employment categories with different approval patterns.
- Customers with higher debt-to-income ratios.
- Impact of payment history on loan outcomes.
- Relationship between income and requested loan amount.

---

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, financial institutions can:

- Strengthen credit assessment for high-risk customer segments.
- Give greater importance to credit score and payment history.
- Monitor customers with high DTI ratios.
- Use credit tiers for better customer segmentation.
- Develop appropriate loan policies based on risk profiles.
- Monitor loan purposes associated with higher risk.
- Use data-driven credit evaluation to reduce potential loan defaults.
